 Borrower Copilot

> An autonomous, client-side rules engine designed to arm Indian borrowers with deterministic financial truth before they step into a bank branch.

Every lender has an underwriting model designed to maximize interest extraction and protect the bank. The borrower has nothing. **Borrower Copilot** bridges that gap. It is a zero-backend self-assessment tool that turns lending judgment into transparent rules, ensuring the borrower is the best-informed person in the room.
